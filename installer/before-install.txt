Local3D turns a photo or a text prompt into a textured 3D model (GLB) on your own NVIDIA graphics card.

This installer is small. On first start Local3D asks before it downloads:

  - the ComfyUI runtime (about 2 GB, official release, checksum-verified)
  - AI model files (about 15 GB, plus about 7 GB if you add Prompt to 3D)

Everything stays on this PC. After the models are downloaded, generation works offline.

You need an NVIDIA graphics card (RTX 20-series or newer; 12 GB or more of video memory is recommended)
and an up-to-date NVIDIA driver.
